# n7

Benchmark protocol 3.5, row N7 (wording ablation of 2.5). Author draft only: not yet blind-labelled, adjudicated,
captured or frozen. Files: `n7.draft.jsonl` (120 items), pages `n7/pages/001.html`-`120.html` (one page per item).

## Purpose

The `presence` site (`wait-for --expect`, no `--text`/`--url`): is what the expectation describes visibly on the
page now. 2.5 asks whether the shipped wording (`W0`, "... The page shows it.") under-reads expectations that name a
kind of content rather than its words, so N7 pairs two item kinds on the same scenarios:

- `abstract`: the expectation names a category only: "a greeting", "a welcome message", "a confirmation",
  "a confirmation message", "a success message", "an error message", "an error", "search results",
  "a list of search results", "a total amount", "a total price".
- `concrete`: the expectation names quoted or specific content: `the message "Order 482117 confirmed"`,
  `the error "..."`, `the greeting "Good morning, Oscar"`, `a confirmation that transfer TR-J4X2P7 was sent`,
  `search results for "warranty"`, `a total of $1,280.00`, `a greeting to Ірина`.

Each item carries `kind` (`abstract`/`concrete`, read by `analyze/verdict.ts` as the 2.5 item kind), `category`
(greeting, confirmation, error, results, total), `condition`, `base`, `template`, `page_kind`, and for concrete items
`expect_form` (`quote` = the page's exact words, English pages only; `specific` = a reference, name, query or amount
described in English).

## Generator

    node tools/bench/sets/gen/n7.mjs

Plain Node, no dependencies, seed `refsure-bench-n7-20261016`. Modules: `gen/n7.mjs` (plan, render, write),
`n7-events.mjs` / `n7-events-c.mjs` (abstract / concrete slot blocks and `why`), `n7-lang.mjs` (greetings, search
states, tip frames, abstract wording), `n7-lib.mjs` (people, near references and amounts), `n7-templates.mjs` and
`n7-tpl-*.mjs` (one per template). Page chrome reuses `n5-shell.mjs`, `n5-lang.mjs` and `n5-lib.mjs` unchanged.
Items are shuffled once (seeded) before ids are given, so labelling batches do not see a base's items side by side.

Checked by a throwaway script: cells 30/30/30/30 and the language split above; at most 2 items per base; 120 distinct
pages, each under 8 KB; no URL, script, image, stylesheet link or `url(` in any page; item URLs only
`https://<host>.example.test/...`; no forbidden company name; every quoted span of every `why` appears in the page text.

## Known limits

- `input` is provisional (`input_provisional: true`): `page` is this generator's text reading and `pageUrl` an
  `example.test` stand-in; the native capture replaces both with what refsure reads through Orca.
- The total category is thin (7 items) and categories are partly tied to templates; read category slices as
  descriptive only.
- Abstract wordings repeat (11 distinct strings); concrete expectations are all different.
- Expectations are English only; translated pages test cross-language reading, not non-English expectations.
- Ukrainian greetings use the vocative ("Вітаємо, Ірино!") while `a greeting to Ірина` names the nominative.
- One author model family (Claude) wrote templates and gold; blind labelling and adjudication are still to run (3.5).

## Blind label and adjudication (2026-10-16)

- Labeler models: `google/gemini-3.1-pro-preview` through OpenRouter for all 120 items, one pass, no retries; `agy`
  (`gemini-3.8-flash-high`, A2) labelled none. Every batch's first `agy` attempt answered `RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED` (429,
  "Individual quota reached", reset in about 2h14m); a reset past A2's 30-minute bound sent every batch to A2's
  OpenRouter fallback (N7 is synthetic, no X4 item). 5 batches of 24, each in its own empty directory holding only its
  blind file and schema; no batch held two items of one base; every batch parsed on the first answer.
- Question: the presence wording of the N9 blind file (the question form of the shipped instructions, criteria text
  verbatim from `wordings.ts`), with the raw page HTML. `n7.blind.jsonl` holds no gold, `why`, `kind`, `condition`
  or note.
- Blind agreement, raw: 119 of 120 (0.9917). By kind x gold: abstract true 30/30, abstract false 30/30, concrete
  true 30/30, concrete false 29/30 (`negated` 6/7). By batch position: 1-6 30/30, 7-12 30/30, 13-18 30/30,
  19-24 29/30.
- Adjudication (`n7.adjudication.md`, a fresh Claude Opus 5.5 agent): keep draft 1, take label 0, drop 0. The one
  disagreement (n7-101, a "No results for “design”" page under `search results for "design"`) is settled by the page
  listing no result; the labeler marked the same sentence false on n7-063. After adjudication the blind labels agree
  with the final gold on the same 119 of 120 (no gold changed); the final gold is settled on 120 of 120.
- Drops (X8): none. `n7.jsonl` is byte-equal to `n7.draft.jsonl` (sha256
  24b842389fedc8d7808a6f2309408d6f93b9fc0a760b332df6ba1d3e8a049887); no item carries `adjudicated`.
- Limit: one labeler, from the fallback model rather than A2's primary, and the adjudicator is the author's model
  family (T3). Near agreement says the gold is clear to a strong reader; it says nothing about whether W0 under-reads
  the abstract items, which is what 2.5 measures.

## Floors (3.6)

- N7 has no row in 3.6 and no danger floor: it feeds only the wording ablation (2.5, Q7, Q8), whose minimum
  detectable effect (about 0.35 per held-out kind x gold cell of ~15, about 0.2 on ~30 per kind x gold) is stated
  for exactly 30 per cell; all four cells keep 30, so that statement holds.
- A1 / P3b's 345 danger floor binds N3-N6 only. N7's 60 false items are not part of N6's pool, and no N7 decision
  touches N6: N6's danger-eligible pool stays 360 (n6.md), >= 345, holds.
