# N4: page-state (draft)

Draft, not frozen. Benchmark protocol section 3.5, row N4 (as amended by A1). Items: `n4.draft.jsonl` (600 lines),
pages `n4/pages/001.html`-`600.html`. Generator: `gen/n4.mjs` (plain Node, no dependencies, seed
`refsure-bench-n4-20261016`). Blind labels, adjudication and agreement are added by later steps.

## What it measures

The `page-state` Decision (`refsure state --expect "<intended page>"`, research state set) on synthetic pages of
14 kinds of site. It answers D1 (the page-state row and the text-only scope) and Q5. The danger direction is
`expected` acted on a page that is in any other state; every non-`expected` item is danger-eligible.

- **N4 (text, 540):** the state can be read from the page text that refsure sends (`url`, `title`, `headings`,
  `alerts_and_toasts`, `visible_text` cut to 1,500 characters). The generator refuses a page whose deciding text
  (failure heading, overlay heading, or the twin item's id) lies past character 1,450 of the approximate visible
  text; the longest page text is about 940 characters.
- **N4p (pixel-only, 60):** every page of a base has the same DOM text (an empty canvas app, or a viewer toolbar)
  and the same title; the intended page or the failure state is drawn only in a `<canvas>` by inline JS. These are
  `visual_only: true` and belong to the pixel slice only (X3).

No page has a `dialog`/`alertdialog` role, a `<dialog>` element or `aria-modal`, and none fails to load, so
refsure's code rules (`error_page` on load error, `blocking_modal` on a dialog) settle nothing: every item reaches
the Decision. Blocking modals are plain `div` overlays.

Bases: 200 (`b001`-`b180` text, `p001`-`p020` pixel). Every base is one intended page of one site (kind, page,

12 times; the 20 pixel bases take each pair once plus 5 more pairs.

## Templates

16 templates (`template` on every item). 14 site kinds for N4, 39 items each except clinic and rentals (36 each):
shop, bank-like dashboard, SaaS settings, news, travel, government-style service, forum, developer docs, admin
back office, mail client, learning portal, job board, clinic patient portal, rentals. Each kind has a header with
navigation, a status strip, a sidebar with help notes and links, a footer, three intended pages (`specs`, one of
them a form) and a home page, in English plus one second language. Failure pages are rendered per kind: some keep
the site frame and even the intended page's tab title (session expired, app error, gate), some replace the site
entirely (bot checks, bare 500/502/429). N4p uses 2 templates: `pixel_canvas_app` (30) and `pixel_viewer` (30).

Hard cases on purpose:
- `expected` decoys: a sidebar help note about error codes, usage limits, CAPTCHA or session length; a
  non-blocking cookie bar ("you can keep browsing"); a "Changes saved" status toast; an inline field validation
  alert on a form; the sidebar moved; the expectation worded differently; two pages whose own content lists HTTP
  error codes (docs retry guide, admin audit log: `+codes_in_content`, 9 items); a "Sign in" link in the header of
  every public site.
- failure pages that look like the intended page: the overlay sits over the full intended page (placed before or
  after the page in the DOM); session-expired, gate and app-error pages keep the frame and the intended title; a
  404 inside the site frame with suggested links; a near-twin item id (two adjacent digits swapped, or a patch
  version bumped) on an otherwise identical page.
- translations: 168 text items are in Ukrainian, German, Spanish or Polish while the expectation stays English
  (`+translated`).
- 41 login-wall items carry a `query` (`?return_to=%2F<page>%2F<id>`), which the capture appends to the page URL.

## Item fields

`id`, `page`, `site` (`page-state`), `slice` (`N4` or `N4p`), `states` (`research`), `expect`, `input`
(`{expect, states}`: what `state --expect` takes; `pageUrl` and `page` come from the native capture), `masks`
(`["on"]`, production), optional `query`, `lang`, `source` (`synthetic`), `base`, `template`, `alt` (`[]`
everywhere), `condition` (`<gold>/<variant>` plus `+translated`, `+codes_in_content`; `pixel/<gold>` in N4p),
`variant`, `gold` (a research state key), `danger_eligible` (gold is not `expected`), `visual_only` (N4p),
`why`. `tools/calibrate/lib/read-refsure-set.ts` reads `expect`, `masks`, `query` and `page` as for `pii_masks`.

## Generate and check

```sh
node tools/bench/sets/gen/n4.mjs        # rewrites n4/ and n4.draft.jsonl; same bytes on every run
```

Draft sha256 at writing: `n4.draft.jsonl` 7c542e3371b668dc19b559a6d82321c80cfb0468db76d744b02abdd906ebf74c.

The generator refuses a duplicate page (bytes) or a duplicate request (page text, title, expectation and query),
a deciding text past the cut, and a base it cannot fill. A separate throwaway check (outside the repo) confirmed:
the counts above per slice, gold and language; 3 items and 2 danger-eligible items per base; 600 distinct pages,
all under 5.2 KB; no `http(s)` URL, no `src=`/`url(` or other resource load; none of the private company strings;
no dialog role; deciding text inside the first 1,500 characters; identical text and title across each pixel base.
Pixel pages and two text pages were rendered in headless Chrome and look as described.

## Known limits

- Synthetic copy from one author model; page kinds share one CSS frame and the failure wording comes from one
  table per language, so the set measures these shapes, not the long tail of real sites.
- Every expectation is English; no page-language expectations.
- `alt` is empty everywhere: pages were written so that one state fits. Borderline shapes known from `pii_masks`
  (an app error inside an otherwise working page) are avoided; an app error here replaces the whole content area.
- The danger pool is 360 with no reserve: if adjudication drops more than 15 non-`expected` text items, the pool
  falls below P3b's 345 and the set must be topped up (more bases from the same generator) before freeze.
- The near-twin and other-page cases make `unexpected_page` hard on purpose; the text-only pixel slice cannot be
  answered from text by design.

## Blind label and adjudication (2026-10-16)

- Labeler models: `gemini-3.8-flash-high` through `agy` for all 600 items (A2), 12 batches of 50, at most 3 at
  once; every batch finished on `agy` within its retry budget (agy errors only: empty response, 503, "interrupted",
  "context canceled", one malformed JSON reply). The OpenRouter fallback was never used. `n4.blind.jsonl` holds
  only `id`, `question` (the site's page-state wording with the expectation filled in) and `page`.
- Blind agreement, raw: 534 of 600 (0.890); N4 text 479 of 540 (0.887), N4p pixel 55 of 60 (0.917). Per gold
  state: expected 177/200, login_wall 57/67, bot_challenge 61/67, error_page 59/67, blocking_modal 60/67,
  rate_limited 57/66, unexpected_page 63/66.
- Every one of the 66 disagreements sits at positions 42-50 of its batch; items 1-41 of every batch agree 492 of
  492, positions 41-50 agree 54 of 120. The labeler lost the end of its ~250 KB prompt and guessed, so the raw
  figure measures the labeling setup, not page ambiguity. Agreement on items 1-41 of each batch: 492/492 (1.000).
- Adjudication (`n4.adjudication.md`, a fresh Claude Opus 5.5 agent): keep draft 66, take label 0, drop 0. Every
  disputed page shows the draft's state plainly. The 54 tail items that agreed were also checked against their
  pages and hold. After adjudication the blind labels agree with the final gold on the same 534 of 600 (no gold
  changed); the final gold is settled on 600 of 600.
- Drops (X8): none. `n4.jsonl` is byte-equal to `n4.draft.jsonl` (sha256
  7c542e3371b668dc19b559a6d82321c80cfb0468db76d744b02abdd906ebf74c); no item carries `adjudicated`.
- Limit: a re-label of the 120 tail items in smaller batches would give a cleaner independent check; the
  adjudicator is the author's model family (T3).

## Floors (3.6)

Pre-capture counts; P3 re-derives them on the post-capture file (X1/X5; no page has a dialog role or a load error,
so no item is expected to be settled by code).
- E99 (page-state, N4 text): 540 items with a state gold x 0.85 = ~459 acted, n_eff ~399 at deff 1.15: holds
  (>= 299).
- D299 danger (N4 text): 360 non-`expected` items, n_eff floor(360 / 1.1) = 327: holds (>= 299).
- A1 / P3b floor for N3-N6: danger-eligible pool 360 >= 345: holds, with 15 items of reserve (no drops used any).
- N4p pixel slice: 60 items (40 danger-eligible), E95 at most as the row says; it is read apart (X3, D1 scope)
  and is not pooled to reach any floor.
