# n8

Benchmark protocol 3.5, row N8 (3.6 notes: E95 at most, P* = 0.95; the 60 `none` items are below D299 and not part
of the verdict). Draft only: not yet blind-labelled, adjudicated, captured or frozen.
Files: `n8.draft.jsonl` (300 items), one page per item under `n8/pages/NNN.html` (NNN = the item id's number).

## Purpose

The `option-map` site (`pick-option --field @eN --want TEXT`): given a dropdown's field line, a `want` in any wording
or language, and the field's option labels, which option means the want, or `none`. Gold is one option, named by
its `value` and visible `text`, or `"none"`. The set is held-out data for the option-map default and row (Q1, Q5).
A wrong pick writes a wrong value into a form (wrong country, plan, currency), so on this site a dangerous act is a
wrong act and safety is the precision rule alone (A1).

## Composition (protocol: 300 fields, 5 languages, `none` gold 60, fold-near decoys)

| gold | condition | items | en | uk | de | es | pl | what makes it hard |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| option | translated | 40 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 | the want is the option's name in another of the five languages (`want_lang`) |
| option | code | 40 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 | an ISO country or currency code, a dialling code or a language tag (`pt-BR`, `zh-Hant`); half carry the gold's look-alikes too |
| option | paraphrase | 40 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 | a description, never the label ("pay the courier in cash when the parcel arrives") |
| option | fold_near | 40 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 | an official-style alias ("Slovak Republic", "British English", "Team plan, billed annually", "version 3.2"); every look-alike of the gold (Slovenia, English (US), Team monthly, 3.12) is listed beside it |
| option | format | 40 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 | the same value written another way: 24 h vs 12 h, spoken times ("halb sieben abends" = 18:30), "GMT+5:30", "half a kilo", "2 Erw. + 1 Ki." |
| option | long_list | 40 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 | the endonym ("Magyarország", "Slovenčina", "Ελληνικά") among 25-49 options sorted by name |
| none | none_absent | 30 | 6 | 6 | 6 | 6 | 6 | the wanted value is simply not offered (label, alias or description) |
| none | none_near | 30 | 6 | 6 | 6 | 6 | 6 | not offered, but its look-alike is (Australia for "Republic of Austria", 3.2.x for "version 3.12", +09:00 for "UTC +9:30", Economy and Business for premium economy) |

Gold option 240, gold `none` 60 (`danger_eligible: true` on exactly those 60). Languages are page languages, 60 each.
`want_lang`: the page language for paraphrase, fold_near, format and the none items; another of the five languages
for translated (always different from the page language; en 6, uk 9, de 9, es 9, pl 7); `code` and `endonym` for
code and long_list.

Every item is built so that refsure's code path cannot settle it: the want never folds equal to an option label
(`pick-option` would pick by code) and no two options fold equal (the twin rule would refuse); the checker
re-verifies both on the pages. Half the fields open with a placeholder option ("Select…", "Bitte wählen", empty
value), which is an option node refsure offers too; 40% of the others show a non-gold option as the current value.

## Bases

A base is one scenario: (page language, template, domain). It holds at most 4 items (here at most 3): 219 bases,
146 with one item, 65 with two, 8 with three (mean 1.37). Every item carries `base` and `template`. Items are
shuffled with a fixed seed before ids are given, so a labelling batch does not see one base's items side by side.

## Item fields

`id, page, site ("option-map"), lang, want_lang, source ("synthetic"), condition, base, template, kind, domain,
widget ("select" | "listbox"), field_ref {selector, role "combobox", name}, input {field, want, options},
input_provisional, gold, gold_index, alt ([]), danger_eligible, why`.

- `input` is refsure's `OptionMapInput` (`field`, `want`, `options`). `want` is final. `options` are every option
  label of the field (placeholder included), ranked as `pick-option` ranks them (content words shared with `want`
  first, ties in page order; `contentWords`/`fold` ported from `src/page-model`). `field` is a provisional v1 line
  (`combobox "<label>" in="main > heading \"…\""`, the sidebar field of docs-page has no `main`). The native capture
  replaces `field` and the option order with what refsure reads through Orca, and `gold_index` with the gold's
  index there (`input_provisional: true`).
- `gold` is `{ value, text }` (the option's `value` / `data-value` and its visible label) or `"none"`; `alt` is
  empty on every item (each gold was built to be the only option that fits).
- `field_ref.selector` (`#f<n>`) is the field the capture hands to `pick-option --field`. Custom listboxes are
  rendered open (`aria-expanded="true"`), so no item needs `--open`.
- `why` is one English sentence naming the field and the gold (or what is missing), checkable against the page.

## Generator

    node tools/bench/sets/gen/n8.mjs

Plain Node, no dependencies, seed `refsure-bench-n8-20261016`; two runs give byte-equal output (sha256 of
`n8.draft.jsonl` checked). Modules: `n8.mjs` (plan, build, write), `n8-want.mjs` (one case per domain and
condition, with the code-path and twin guards), `n8-domains.mjs` (registry, conditions per domain), `n8-dom-*.mjs`
(vocabulary per domain, five languages), `n8-page.mjs` (field widgets, page), `n8-templates.mjs` and
`n8-tpl-*.mjs` (15 templates), `n8-lib.mjs` (fold, content words, ranking, provisional field line). It reuses
`n5-lib.mjs` (seeded rng, escaping) and `n5-shell.mjs` (page chrome and looks); a change there changes these pages.

Checked by a throwaway script (outside the repo): counts per condition and language equal the row above; no base
over 4; no two pages equal; no external URL, `src=`, stylesheet link or `url(` and none of the forbidden company
names; every gold's value and text sit in the item's field on its page and at `gold_index` of `input.options`;
`input.options` equal the page's options; no option folds equal to the want or to another option; every page
under 8 KiB.

## Known limits

- The plan rotates domains per condition, so a domain's share follows how many conditions it can author:
  country 53 and language 42 items, the smallest domains 4-8. Travel-booking hosts the most domains (56 items).
- `alt` is always empty. A few golds rest on a reading a careful person makes but a hasty one might not:
  "Slovenčina" is Slovak (not Slovenian), "halb sieben" is 18:30, "Republic of Korea" is South Korea, a "Vegetarian
  (with dairy and eggs)" option is a near miss for a vegan want. These are the intended hard cases; the blind label
  and adjudication decide them.
- Endonyms in long_list are limited to well-known ones (Latin, Greek, Cyrillic or a common script); a few repeat
  across page languages (country and language pools are small).
- none_absent is the easier `none` class (the wanted value is plainly missing); none_near carries the danger.
- uk, de, es and pl copy was written by the author, not reviewed by a native speaker.
- 60 `none` items are below D299 by design (3.6: option-map is experimental, no verdict rides on its danger cell).
- Blind agreement and adjudication results are added here after P1 (3.5).

## Blind labelling and adjudication (P1)

- Labeler: `gemini-3.8-flash-high` through `agy` for all 300 items (A2); OpenRouter was never used. 12 batches of
  25, each in its own empty directory, at most 3 at once. Blind file `n8.blind.jsonl` (id, question, page only).
- Raw blind agreement: 299 / 300 (99.67%). Gold option 239 / 240, gold `none` 60 / 60. Every condition 100% except
  paraphrase 39 / 40; every language 100% except pl 59 / 60. No drift by batch position (positions 1-5: 59 / 60,
  the rest 60 / 60). The labeler flagged 2 items ambiguous (n8-148, n8-263); both agree with gold.
- Adjudication (`n8.adjudication.md`, fresh Claude Opus 5.5 agent): 1 disagreement (n8-025), kept the draft gold.
  0 labels taken, 0 drops (X8). Agreement after adjudication: 300 / 300 settled, final gold = draft gold on all 300.
- Final set: `n8.jsonl` (equal to `n8.draft.jsonl`; no item changed, so no `adjudicated` field is set).

### Floors (3.6)

Option-map has no danger floor that binds: its 60 `none` items are below D299 by design and not part of the verdict
(A1; E95 at most, P* = 0.95). A1's 345 danger-eligible floor applies to N3, N4, N5 and N6 only, so it does not touch
N8. The held-out pool stays 240 gold-option + 60 `none` as in 3.6; nothing was dropped, so the row holds.
